<div class="case-block"><span>01 — FRAME</span><h2>Write the decision before the test.</h2><p>Define the treatment, unit of randomization, primary outcome, guardrails, minimum detectable effect, and decision rule before looking at results.</p></div>

<div class="case-block"><span>02 — WATCH</span><h2>Assumptions are part of the result.</h2><p>Interference, novelty, sample-ratio mismatch, repeated peeking, and heterogeneous effects can matter more than the final confidence interval.</p></div></div>
