<div class="case-study"><div class="eyebrow">01 / METRIC SYSTEMS</div><h1>Make a complex business legible.</h1><p class="case-dek">A metric system should do more than report what happened. It should help teams understand why, identify what matters, and decide what to do next.</p>
<div class="case-block"><span>01 — START</span><h2>From metric sprawl to a hierarchy.</h2><p>Start with the business outcome, map the user behavior that creates it, and define leading indicators plus guardrails. Composite scores should simplify decisions—not hide important dimensions.</p></div>
<div class="metric-diagram"><div>BUSINESS OUTCOME</div><i>↓</i><div>USER VALUE</div><i>↓</i><div>DRIVERS</div><i>↓</i><div>OPERATIONAL SIGNALS</div></div>
<div class="case-block"><span>02 — PRINCIPLE</span><h2>Every metric needs a decision.</h2><p>If a metric cannot change a decision, it is probably a report rather than a product metric.</p></div></div>
